## 🎯 Introduction: How Your Brain Will Master AI-102

### The Five Memory Powers You'll Use:

1. **Memory Palace** 🏰 - Your brain remembers places better than facts
2. **Mnemonics** 🎵 - Turn boring lists into catchy phrases
3. **Chunking** 🧩 - Break big concepts into bite-sized pieces
4. **Spaced Repetition** 📅 - Review at perfect intervals
5. **Active Recall** 💪 - Test yourself to strengthen memory

### Why This Works:
Your brain evolved to remember:
- **Where things are** (locations/places)
- **Stories** (narratives with emotion)
- **Patterns** (rhythms, songs, rhymes)
- **Images** (visual memories)

We'll use ALL of these to make AI-102 unforgettable!
